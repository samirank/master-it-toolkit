Local (LocalWP)
Official download: https://localwp.com/
Documentation: https://localwp.com/help-docs/getting-started/installing-local/

Install Local for your operating system and create a new local WordPress site.
Choose the PHP and web-server environment needed by your project; start the site and open WP Admin.
Use the site shell for bundled WP-CLI commands. Export a backup before importing or syncing an existing site.
