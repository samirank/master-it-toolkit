MASTER IT TOOLKIT — START HERE

Standalone package: extract once beside the MASTER-IT-TOOLKIT folder.
Open Start-Windows.exe on Windows x64, Start-Linux.sh on Linux x64,
or Start-macOS.command on an Apple Silicon Mac. Keep the launcher running.
The all-platforms ZIP includes all three supported runtimes on one SSD.
Use runtime-only ZIPs to add a platform to an existing, updated toolkit;
do not overwrite your workspace with a complete fresh package.

The setup wizard checks the drive, helps encrypt your workspace and records
an optional backup plan. It never formats the drive or enables backups for you.
Download tools while online, then scan before taking the SSD offline.
Windows programs cannot run natively on Linux or macOS.

PRIVATE DATA
Set up Private vault with a passphrase and save the recovery key separately.
To trust your own computer, enter the passphrase in the vault dialog and choose
Make this a master computer. You can register multiple OS accounts. The local
credential stays in the OS credential store; the SSD contains an encrypted grant.
Other computers require your passphrase or recovery key. Your shared notes are
available after unlocking, and are not deleted when moving between computers.
Master accounts unlock at startup; Lock now stays locked until you unlock or
restart. Inactivity locks the vault after 15 minutes. Missing/locked credential
stores fall back to passphrase unlock. Use the vault dialog to remove masters.
Separate older copies and backups retain their previous access settings.

SQLite notes, favorites, settings and history are encrypted when you configure
the vault. Exported reports, downloaded tools and browser profiles are separate.
Do not keep sensitive or long-term records here; export them to a permanent home.
Never record customer passwords, recovery codes or authentication secrets.

Source ZIP users: Python 3.11+, cryptography and keyring are required for the
launcher/vault; Playwright and Chromium enable the managed browser. Direct HTML
is static browsing only. See README.md in the repository for current instructions.
Authorized systems only. No authentication bypass or unattended remote access
is configured automatically. Bootable ISO support is a separate future workflow;
normal toolkit use does not require installing Ventoy or formatting your drive.
